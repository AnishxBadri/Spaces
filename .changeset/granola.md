Granola notes reach the meetings they were taken in. Each member pastes their own Granola key on You → Meeting notes and ticks the folders to share; every 15 minutes the notes in those folders are matched to the calendar meeting, or filed as a call, and a note outside them is never read. Moving a note out of the ticked folders, unticking a folder or deleting the note in Granola removes it from Spaces on the next sync, and Disconnect removes everything the key brought in. Typed notes and the transcript are fetched only when the member opts in. Needs sdk ^1.5.
